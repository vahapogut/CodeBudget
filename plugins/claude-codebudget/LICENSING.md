# Free use and commercial permission

CodeBudget is free to use. Starting with **0.1.0-beta.2**, new first-party material is offered under the [CodeBudget Free Use License 1.0](LICENSE). There is no paid CodeBudget tier, license fee, registration requirement or license server. Optional third-party IDE subscriptions and model/provider services have their own terms and costs; CodeBudget's free license does not provide those services.

The policy allows personal and internal business use and requires **prior written permission from [vahapogut](https://github.com/vahapogut)** to offer CodeBudget, modified versions or its functionality as part of a commercial product or service. Permission is not a paid checkout and does not imply a fee. The binding definitions and exceptions are in LICENSE.

## Examples

| Activity | Current license policy |
| --- | --- |
| Use the CLI/plugin privately, at school or for research | Free |
| Use it inside a company, including on proprietary code | Free |
| Use it while developing a commercial app or delivering client work | Free; the resulting app does not inherit CodeBudget's license just because you used the tool |
| Run a private instance for your own employees and contractors | Free |
| Share an independent, free community fork | Allowed with the license and notices; modifications must be identified |
| Sell a rebranded version or bundle it into a paid app/plugin | Prior written permission required |
| Offer customers its functionality through commercial SaaS, API or managed hosting | Prior written permission required |
| Include it in the free tier, trial or promotional bundle of a commercial offering | Prior written permission required |
| Independently use third-party dependencies or legacy Apache material | Their own licenses apply |

For a permission request, open a licensing issue in the [project repository](https://github.com/vahapogut/CodeBudget/issues) describing who will offer the product, what CodeBudget material or functionality it includes, and how it will be distributed or hosted. Do not include confidential information. A request is not permission: obtain an express written grant from vahapogut before proceeding. Permission can cover only rights the person granting it actually controls; separately licensed contributions may require their rights holders' permission.

## Prior Apache-2.0 releases remain usable

The repository was publicly distributed under Apache-2.0 before this policy change. Its last Apache checkpoint is **61c53510e082e7830ccbe4b67b61e81cdf53c1ea**, dated 2026-09-29, with package version **0.1.0-beta.1**. The exact former license is preserved in [LEGACY_LICENSE](LEGACY_LICENSE).

Apache-2.0 grants existing recipients irrevocable copyright permissions, subject to its terms. Changing the license on the current branch cannot withdraw those grants, erase public copies or prevent commercial use of that previously licensed material. Unchanged legacy material remains usable under Apache-2.0 even when copied from a later checkout; a version bump is not a retroactive restriction. New protectable contributions offered only under the current license are subject to the new terms. Git history remains intact. See the [Apache-2.0 grant and redistribution conditions](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

The original synthetic/captured reducer fixtures and thirty pilot task definitions, starter sources and reference solutions retain their explicit Apache-2.0 licensing. Existing task/fixture metadata is preserved. Third-party dependencies and their license inventory are unchanged. No commercial restriction in the new license overrides these separate grants.

## Source availability and contribution rights

The source can be viewed and used under the stated permissions, but the commercial-offering restriction means this is **source-available**, not OSI-approved open source. The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) requires permission for commercial fields of use.

Contributors retain ownership and offer new contributions under the current license unless a separate agreement is made. This does not assign their copyright or automatically let the project owner sell or relicense their work. See contribution expectations in the source repository's CONTRIBUTING.md. This is a custom license; no independent legal review or universal enforceability is claimed.
